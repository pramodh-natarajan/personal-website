About

Hey there, I'm Pramodh. I'm a builder and tech leader, currently CTO & Head of AI at Pipeline AI, where we're figuring out how AI can help revenue teams find and win the right customers.

My path here has zigzagged happily, though the map makes sense in hindsight. It started in Chennai: a computer science degree at SSN, Musicperk, a music startup I co-founded in my final year, and a first job at Ford. In 2014, I moved to the US for a master's at Arizona State, where I also researched intelligent tutoring systems. Then came a decade in the Bay Area: building game platforms at Electronic Arts from 2016, data pipelines at LiveRamp from 2021, and a Berkeley Haas MBA finished alongside work. In 2026, I moved back to Bengaluru with my family and stepped into the CTO role at Pipeline AI.

Outside work, I'm an animal lover, vegan for ethical reasons, and an unapologetic public-transit and city-planning nerd.
